# Content Sources & Research Guidelines: Khmer Heritage

## 1. Academic & Institutional Sources Priority
All historical, architectural, and cultural claims must derive from verified and reputable sources:
1. **Academic & Research Bodies**:
   - École française d'Extrême-Orient (EFEO)
   - APSARA National Authority (Authority for the Protection and Management of Angkor and the Region of Siem Reap)
   - Royal University of Fine Arts (RUFA), Phnom Penh
   - UNESCO World Heritage Centre
   - National Museum of Cambodia
   - Center for Khmer Studies (CKS)
2. **Scholarly Works & Historians**:
   - George Cœdès, Lawrence Palmer Briggs, Claude Jacques, David Chandler, Henri Marchal, Bernard-Philippe Groslier.
3. **Official Cultural Archives**:
   - Ministry of Culture and Fine Arts (Cambodia)
   - Buddhist Institute of Cambodia (Institut Bouddhique)

## 2. Prohibition of Unverified Internet Sources
- Unverified blogs, general social media posts, unmoderated forum entries, and non-authoritative commercial websites are strictly prohibited as primary factual citations.

## 3. Media Sources
- Wikimedia Commons (verified public domain or CC-licensed media).
- Institutional open-access digital repositories (e.g. Gallica / BnF, Library of Congress, British Library, Met Museum Open Access).
- Commissioned or direct field photography with explicit licensing.
