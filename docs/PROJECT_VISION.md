# Project Vision: Khmer Heritage

## 1. Executive Summary
**Khmer Heritage** is a digital encyclopedia and cultural discovery platform dedicated to preserving, organizing, and presenting the rich heritage, history, and culture of the Khmer civilization.

## 2. Core Vision & Experience
- **Paradigm**: `DISCOVER → EXPLORE → LEARN → DISCOVER SOMETHING RELATED`
- Unlike a standard blog or flat static listing, Khmer Heritage connects related cultural topics, historical eras, artifacts, monuments, rituals, and figures into a navigable cultural discovery graph.
- Users can begin reading about a specific topic (e.g., Angkor Wat or Apsara dance), inspect curated media and factual citations, and effortlessly explore interconnected subjects (e.g., Jayavarman VII, Bayon style, Reamker epic, Pinpeat orchestra).

## 3. Cultural Pillars Covered
- **Khmer History**: Pre-Angkor (Funan, Chenla), Angkor Empire, Post-Angkor, Modern Era.
- **Architecture & Temples**: Angkor Wat, Bayon, Banteay Srei, Sambor Prei Kuk, Preah Vihear, etc.
- **Arts & Sculpture**: Apsara, Kbach motifs, stone & bronze carvings, bas-reliefs, mural arts.
- **Music & Performance**: Traditional music, Pinpeat & Mohori ensembles, classical dance, shadow puppetry (Sbek Thom).
- **Musical Instruments**: Roneat, Tro, Chapei Dong Veng, Sampho, Khloy, etc.
- **Traditions, Rituals & Festivals**: Pchum Ben, Khmer New Year (Choul Chnam Thmey), Water Festival (Bon Om Touk).
- **Clothing & Textiles**: Hol, Phamung, Krama, traditional wedding & royal attire.
- **Language & Epigraphy**: Khmer script (Aksar Mul, Aksar Chrieng), ancient inscriptions, literature (Reamker).
- **Culinary & Agriculture**: Traditional cuisine, ancestral cooking techniques, fermented foods, Tonle Sap traditions.
- **Crafts & Trades**: Silk weaving, silver craftsmanship, pottery, lacquerware, stone carving.
- **Places & Geography**: Historical provinces, sacred mountains (Phnom Kulen), waterways.
- **Historical Figures & Kings**: Kings, scholars, artists, master musicians, cultural preservers.
- **Mythology & Beliefs**: Buddhist traditions, Brahmanical/Hindu syncretism, Neak Ta, animist folklore.

## 4. Multi-Platform Delivery
- **Target Platforms**: Android Application, iOS Application, and Web Platform.
- **Development Priority**: Mobile-first (Android + iOS), with web sharing identical data/content layer.
- **Single Content Source**: Mobile apps and website consume the exact same structured data payloads. Updating content once automatically updates all platforms.

## 5. Cost & Sustainability Principles
- Prioritize free tier, open-source, and low-operating-cost architecture.
- Cloudflare R2 is the designated primary storage for structured content, media, and metadata.
